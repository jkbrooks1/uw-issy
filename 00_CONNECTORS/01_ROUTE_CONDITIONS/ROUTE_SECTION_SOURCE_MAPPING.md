## Issaquah-approach street list (corrected, canonical GPX v2 — supersedes the v1-GPX-derived list below)

**CORRECTION (fourth follow-up cycle, 2026-07-28):** the project owner supplied a corrected canonical GPX (`v2.UnivWA-Issaquah.gpx`, now installed as the canonical `data/route/UnivWA-Issaquah.gpx`; the prior version is archived at `data/route/archive/UnivWA-Issaquah.v1.20260728.gpx`, not deleted). The fix replaces an incorrect mid-route jog through **Bear Creek Trail** and **Northeast 65th Street** (near Redmond, NOT Issaquah — this was already mislabeled as "Issaquah-approach" in the prior version of this document, an error independent of the GPX itself) with a direct **Marymoor Connector Trail** path that actually routes through Marymoor Park before rejoining the East Lake Sammamish Trail. Route distance changed from 33.49 mi to 33.83 mi; bounding box is unchanged (lat 47.55207-47.75889, lon -122.3057 to -122.04414).

**Northeast 65th Street and the first "East Lake Sammamish Parkway Northeast" jog are REMOVED from the route entirely** and must be dropped from ISS-01's keyword/street-name filter list — they were never actually part of the Issaquah approach (they sat ~15 miles north, near Redmond/Bear Creek) and are no longer part of the corrected route at all.

For ISS-01's route-impact keyword/street-name filtering, the exact streets the route's real Issaquah-approach segment uses (verified against the corrected v2 GPX) are:

- East Lake Sammamish Parkway Northeast (the segment near ~47.615-47.625 lat, south of Sammamish, leading into Issaquah)
- East Lake Sammamish Lane Northeast
- East Lake Sammamish Trail (the trail itself, terminus approach)

**Marymoor Connector Trail** (new named trail segment, corrected GPX only) should be added to the named-trail-segment matching list for the Marymoor Park section (segment 7) — likely King County Parks-managed as part of the Marymoor Park trail system, same as the Sammamish River Trail and East Lake Sammamish Trail it connects; ownership not yet independently confirmed and should be verified in a future cycle rather than assumed.

Any ISS-01 Civic Alert whose text names one of these exact streets should be flagged CONFIRMED_ROUTE_IMPACT-candidate (still subject to manual review, per the free-text nature of the source); alerts naming other Issaquah streets not on this list should default to NOT_ROUTE_RELEVANT unless a human reviewer determines otherwise.

## WSDOT-01 confirmed crossing zones (derived from GPX cross-referenced against WSDOT/city project documentation)

1. **SR-522 (Bothell Way) through Kenmore** — corresponds to the Burke-Gilman – north Lake WA/Kenmore segment (segment 3 above, roughly GPX waypoints in the 47.66-47.74 lat range along the lakefront). Confirmed via the City of Kenmore's WSDOT-funded "Burke Gilman/SR522 Accessibility Project" page (existing pedestrian underpass at 73rd Ave NE; signed at-grade SR-522 crossing used in official King County trail detours).
2. **I-405/SR-522 interchange, Bothell/Woodinville boundary** — corresponds to the Sammamish River Trail – Bothell/Woodinville segment (segment 5 above, roughly GPX waypoints 19-21, ~47.752-47.758 lat, -122.20 to -122.21 lon). Confirmed via a May 2025 trail-condition report describing flagger-controlled trail crossings during active interchange-area construction.
3. **I-90 near Issaquah-Preston Trail junction** — NOT a confirmed on-route crossing. This interchange is east of the supplied GPX's terminus (~47.552, -122.044); documented as corridor context only, not claimed as an on-route WSDOT crossing.

KC-06 (`RoadAlerts/KingCo_Road_Alerts`) is real, live, and structurally excellent (fielded data + geometry), but scoped only to unincorporated King County roads. This route runs almost entirely through incorporated cities, so KC-06 is not mapped to any specific segment above pending a proper line-buffer intersection test against the full GPX (only a bounding-box test was performed this cycle, which is known to be unreliable for this source — see SOURCE_REGISTRY.json).
